Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect customer information.

Last updated: May 11, 2026

Information We Collect

Radiant Med Spa collects customer information such as name, phone number, email, appointment details, and message history when submitted through forms, booking requests, phone calls, or SMS.

How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to inquiries, schedule appointments, send appointment-related messages, missed-call follow-ups, customer care messages, reminders, and review requests.

SMS Consent and Privacy

SMS consent is used only for Radiant Med Spa customer communication. Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Users can opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP and can request help by replying HELP. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies based on customer interactions and appointment activity.

Information Sharing

Radiant Med Spa may use service providers to operate its website, booking tools, phone systems, and customer communication systems. These providers are only permitted to use information as needed to provide services for Radiant Med Spa.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Radiant Med Spa at hello@radiantmedspa.com or (555) 123-4567.
